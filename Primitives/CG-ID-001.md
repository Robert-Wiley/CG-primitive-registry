# CG-ID-001: Vibe Signature Card (VSC)

## 1. Primitive Identity
- Primitive ID: CG-ID-001
- Primitive Name: Vibe Signature Card (VSC)
- Primitive Type (Level): Identity Primitive
- Primary Layer: Cross-Layer
- Status: Validated

## 2. Primitive Orientation
- Layer: Cross-Layer, identity constraints apply across truth, execution, and delegation
- Level: Identity Primitive
- Vector(s):
  - Preserves intent coherence across time, context, and delegation
  - Constrains action pre-execution, before automation begins
  - Aligns behavior, outputs, and decisions to a declared identity

## 3. Discovery Context
- Problem space: Ensuring governance systems maintain consistent intent and values as complexity increases
- Trigger moment: Recognizing that identity must constrain agents and processes before they act
- What existing models failed to explain: How to preserve intent and alignment without relying solely on policy, training, or after-the-fact review

## 4. Canonical Definition
A governance primitive that encodes identity as a constraint mechanism, shaping intent, boundaries, and acceptable behavior before execution or delegation occurs.

## 5. Irreducibility Test
If this primitive is removed:
- Intent becomes unstable across contexts, tools, and delegated actors
- Delegated systems drift toward local optimization, or convenience, rather than coherent identity
- Governance alignment degrades because meaning and action are no longer anchored to a stable identity constraint

## 6. Authority Boundaries
This primitive controls:
- Identity-bound constraints that shape what is acceptable before action, execution, or delegation

This primitive forbids:
- Delegated actors or tools acting without identity constraints when identity is required for safety or coherence
- Post hoc rationalization replacing pre-declared intent boundaries

This primitive constrains:
- The set of acceptable actions, tone, and decision boundaries based on declared identity

## 7. Dependency and Interaction Map
- Depends on: A defined identity statement, and a mechanism to apply identity constraints to systems and workflows
- Constrained by: Governance primitives that restrict how identity may be applied, changed, and audited
- Constrains: Delegation layers, and orchestration logic, especially in Agentic AI contexts

## 8. Non-Goals
This primitive does not replace policy, risk analysis, legal compliance, or technical controls. It constrains alignment, it does not compute risk.
